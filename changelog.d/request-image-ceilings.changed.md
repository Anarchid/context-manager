- The live window's image limits stay within the request ceilings, in both strategies' strip, the autobiographical strategy's image watermark and its post-strip estimates. They bind the images the window counts: top-level `image` blocks, by the length of their base64 `source.data`. A `maxLiveImages` or `maxLiveImageBytes` of 0, which meant unlimited, now means up to `maxRequestImages` or `maxRequestImageBytes`. A limit above its ceiling keeps the ceiling's worth, and a fractional limit is floored, to at least 1. The default window, 6 images and 20 MiB, is under the default ceilings and doesn't change.
- A `maxCompressionImageBytes` that isn't a positive finite number (0, a negative, `NaN` or `Infinity`) now means up to `maxRequestImageBytes`. It had meant no budget, or for `NaN`, that every image was replaced.
- Each strategy warns once, when it initializes, when a limit is above its ceiling (the compression budget included), or when a ceiling isn't 0 or a finite number above it and takes its default. A setting the host didn't give is marked as the default.
- `maxLiveImageBytes`' documentation no longer says the API's request cap is 32 MB. It names membrane's request byte cap, which the image byte ceiling leaves room under.
