- The live window's image limits stay within the image ceilings, in both strategies' strip, the autobiographical strategy's image watermark and its post-strip estimates. They bind the images the window counts: top-level `image` blocks, by the length of their base64 `source.data`. A limit above its ceiling keeps the ceiling's worth, and a fractional limit is floored, to at least 1. The default window, 6 images and 20 MiB, is under the default ceilings and doesn't change.
- Each strategy warns once, when it initializes, when a limit is above its ceiling (the compression budget included), or when a ceiling isn't 0 or a finite number above it and takes its default. A setting the host didn't give is marked as the default. Under a window limit of 0 or above a ceiling, it also says once which ceiling it was and what it held, at the first render a select returns where images are shown as placeholders because an image passed that ceiling, rather than because of a limit the host set. That's the image past the ceiling, unless the depth limit, the hysteresis watermark or a limit the host has in force under its ceiling strips it anyway, or the images the hysteresis watermark strips when an image past the ceiling moves it, unless the host's own limits, the depth limit included, would strip them too.
- `maxLiveImageBytes`' documentation no longer says the API's request cap is 32 MB. It names membrane's request byte cap, which the image byte ceiling leaves room under.
